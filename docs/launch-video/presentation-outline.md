# YouTube Pro launch presentation

## Format

- **Length:** 7 slides
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9
- **Use:** Short on-camera presentation and visual chapter cards inside a five-minute product demo
- **Visual language:** Near-black background, warm coral accent, soft gray surfaces, white typography, rounded cards, current product screenshots
- **Rule:** One message per slide, no more than three short supporting lines

## Narrative arc

Start with the finished outcome, name the context-loss problem, show the evidence-aware Research and AI workflow, move into creation, then close with privacy boundaries and a simple invitation to follow the release.

## Slide plan

### Slide 1, “From topic to ready to record”

- **Goal:** Establish the full promise in one sentence.
- **On-screen copy:**
  - Research
  - Decide
  - Write
  - Package
- **Visual:** YouTube Pro logo plus a narrow montage of Research, teleprompter, and thumbnail screens.
- **Speaker note:** “This is YouTube Pro, one connected workflow from public research to a recordable and packaged video.”
- **Time:** 8 seconds

### Slide 2, “The context disappears between tools”

- **Goal:** Make the problem immediately recognizable.
- **On-screen copy:**
  - Too many tabs
  - Generic AI prompts
  - Repeated setup
- **Visual:** A simple broken flow, Search → Notes → AI → Script → Thumbnail, with small gaps between stages.
- **Speaker note:** “Creators already have tools. What they lose is the context when they move between them.”
- **Time:** 12 seconds

### Slide 3, “Research you can inspect”

- **Goal:** Show that the product starts with visible public evidence.
- **On-screen copy:**
  - Up to 50 public videos
  - Sample-aware metrics
  - Every source visible
- **Visual:** `docs/images/research-analytics.png`, followed by a quick crop of `docs/images/research-source-videos.png`.
- **Speaker note:** “The dashboard summarizes the returned sample, shows its coverage, and keeps every source video available for review.”
- **Time:** 35 seconds plus live demo

### Slide 4, “AI that labels what it knows”

- **Goal:** Differentiate the evidence contract from generic AI research.
- **On-screen copy:**
  - Observed
  - Inferred
  - Requires Studio
- **Visual:** `docs/images/research-ai-insights.png`, with three compact status chips.
- **Speaker note:** “Public observations, hypotheses, and owner-only metrics are separated so recommendations do not masquerade as facts.”
- **Time:** 35 seconds plus live demo

### Slide 5, “A script built to be spoken”

- **Goal:** Show the handoff from selected idea to production.
- **On-screen copy:**
  - Grounded idea
  - Editable script
  - Built-in teleprompter
- **Visual:** `docs/images/script-teleprompter.png` with the playback controls in focus.
- **Speaker note:** “The selected promise becomes an editable script and then a focused teleprompter, without leaving the workflow.”
- **Time:** 30 seconds plus live demo

### Slide 6, “Package the promise”

- **Goal:** Complete the workflow with thumbnail creation and resumability.
- **On-screen copy:**
  - Minimal visual brief
  - Optional references
  - Resume recent workflows
- **Visual:** `docs/images/thumbnail-creator.png` beside a small Recent Workflows sidebar crop.
- **Speaker note:** “The same idea promise continues into the thumbnail, while recent workflows keep the project available locally.”
- **Time:** 30 seconds plus live demo

### Slide 7, “Public evidence. Human judgment. One workflow.”

- **Goal:** Close with an honest position and direct CTA.
- **On-screen copy:**
  - Local-first
  - Evidence-aware
  - Wider release coming
- **CTA:** Subscribe and comment **YouTube Pro** for release updates.
- **Visual:** Logo, repository-style dark background, one warm coral CTA.
- **Speaker note:** “It is not a guaranteed-growth button. It is an independent project and a clearer path from public evidence to a creative decision you are ready to execute.”
- **Time:** 15 seconds
