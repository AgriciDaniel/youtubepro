# YouTube Pro five-minute shot list

## Recording setup

- Record at 1920×1080 or higher, 30 fps.
- Use a clean browser profile with notifications, bookmarks, personal tabs, and credentials hidden.
- Use a prepared research workflow with stable public results so the visual story does not depend on live provider timing.
- Keep browser zoom at 100 percent unless a planned close-up is required.
- Move the cursor deliberately, then stop it outside the focal content.
- Record voice separately when possible. It makes cuts, pauses, and error recovery much easier.

## Demo data guidance

Choose a topic that produces visually varied videos and a clear creative opportunity. Avoid a one-word query such as “YouTube,” because broad results make the analysis feel generic. Good demo patterns include:

- A defined creator problem, such as “AI tools for solo YouTube creators”
- A comparison, such as “local AI video editing vs cloud tools”
- A format trend, such as “faceless documentary channels 2026”

Use a previously saved workflow for the final recording. If live generation is shown, film it as a short insert and keep a completed result ready as the recovery shot.

## Visual emphasis

- Zoom or crop into one decision at a time.
- Use chapter cards only at major transitions.
- Favor product motion over animated decoration.
- Keep text overlays to six words or fewer.
- Use the warm coral accent for the current action, blue for AI inference, green for observed evidence, and amber for limitations.

## Claims to avoid

- “This analyzes the YouTube algorithm.”
- “This predicts what will go viral.”
- “This includes private YouTube Analytics.”
- “This guarantees better views or click-through rate.”
- “This is already open source.”
- “Reference images are stored with every project.”

## Pre-publish review

- Confirm no API key, account identifier, private browser data, or local filesystem path is visible.
- Confirm every spoken product claim matches the current build.
- Confirm the repository and license status stated in the CTA are current on recording day.
- Confirm generated and third-party visual assets are permitted for distribution.
- Include “Independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google” in the narration or description.
- Watch once with sound off for visual comprehension.
- Listen once without the screen for narrative comprehension.
- Review the final export on a phone for text size, crop, pacing, and motion comfort.
