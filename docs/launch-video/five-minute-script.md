# YouTube Pro launch video script

## Production target

- **Working title:** This YouTube Tool Shows Its Evidence Before Giving You Ideas
- **Core promise:** Turn a topic into research, evidence-aware ideas, a recordable script, and a thumbnail in one continuous workspace.
- **Audience:** Creators, YouTube strategists, researchers, and builders who are tired of moving context between disconnected tools.
- **Target runtime:** Approximately 5 minutes, including short screen-recording pauses.
- **Delivery:** Warm, direct, curious, and practical. Speak to one creator, not a conference room.

## Teleprompter script

### 0:00-0:20, cold open

Most AI tools tell you what video to make before they show you what they looked at. YouTube Pro starts with the evidence.

Give it one topic, and it carries a public YouTube snapshot into traceable insights, grounded ideas, a script, and a thumbnail, all in one workflow.

Let me show you the complete workflow in about five minutes.

### 0:20-0:50, the problem

Most creator workflows are fragmented. You search in one tab, collect numbers in another, ask an AI for ideas without enough context, move a draft into a document, and start the thumbnail from zero.

The problem is not a lack of tools. The problem is that the context disappears between them.

YouTube Pro keeps it connected from Research to Script Writer to Thumbnail Creator.

### 0:50-1:40, research snapshot

I will start with a topic. I can choose an upload window, duration, and sort order, then run the search.

The public YouTube Data API builds a snapshot of up to fifty returned videos, with views, likes, comments, duration, publishing time, channel information, captions availability, tags, and other available metadata.

Now I can scan the overview instead of opening fifty tabs. I can compare median performance, inspect directional momentum as views per day, review publishing recency, identify recurring tags, and see how complete the public data is.

Every source video remains visible, so the sample is never hidden behind one score.

### 1:40-2:35, AI Insights

Next, Gemini analyzes the exact active snapshot and its aggregates.

The important part is how the result is labeled. Observed findings come from the public sample. Inferences are useful hypotheses, not measured facts. Impressions, click-through rate, traffic sources, and retention are marked as requiring YouTube Studio.

The scan-first view summarizes the research lens, audience questions, opportunities, and next moves. When I need to verify a recommendation, I can open the evidence ledger and trace it to source videos or a clearly labeled aggregate inference.

This gives me direction without pretending the public API knows everything.

### 2:35-3:05, grounded ideas

Once the Insights are valid, the app generates grounded ideas automatically.

Each idea carries a promise, format, audience, evidence, and test rule. I select the strongest direction and send that context forward instead of giving the script generator an empty prompt.

### 3:05-3:55, Script Writer and teleprompter

In Script Writer, the selected idea becomes a complete draft. I choose the format, audience, and delivery traits, then add any constraints that matter.

The result is editable, but the main experience is intentionally simple: a real teleprompter.

I can change pace and text size, press Play or Space to pause, reset, edit, and undo or redo. The script stays connected to the same research and idea context, including when I regenerate a section.

This is where the research becomes something ready to say on camera.

### 3:55-4:25, Thumbnail Creator

From there, I can carry the selected promise into Thumbnail Creator.

I describe the outcome, add short optional text, and open advanced controls only when I need them. I can add permitted references and generate a sixteen-by-nine thumbnail through Gemini.

It is a minimal brief with enough control for a clear, readable package.

### 4:25-4:45, recent workflows and privacy

New Workflow creates a separate local project. My eight most recent workflows stay in this browser, so I can reopen the research, script, or thumbnail where I left it.

API keys stay on the local server. Uploaded reference images are intentionally not retained.

### 4:45-5:05, honest close and CTA

YouTube Pro is not a magic growth button, and it does not access private YouTube Analytics. It turns public evidence into a clearer creative decision, then carries that decision through production.

This is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google. It is currently under private review while I prepare a wider release. If you want to try it or help shape it, subscribe and tell me which niche I should test next.

This is YouTube Pro, from research to something ready to record.
